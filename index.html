Features that Elevate your Audio

🎧

11-Band Equalizer

Drag vertical sliders to boost or cut any frequency band. Switch between Peaking, Shelves, High/Low pass, and Bandpass filters on the fly.

🎛️

Dynamics & Space

Built-in Preamp, Compressor, and Brickwall Limiter. Add transient punch, or widen the stereo image with Mid-Side matrix controls and Reverb.

📈

Real-Time Visualizer

See exactly what you're doing. The frequency response curve visually aggregates all your EQ filters and updates instantly as you tweak.

💾

Preset System

Comes with 9 built-in standard presets. Save and manage your own custom presets locally without needing any accounts or cloud sync.

🛡️

Privacy First

No tracking, no analytics, no external servers. Everything runs entirely within your browser's local sandbox, keeping your data yours.

⚡

Zero Latency

Hooks directly into the native Web Audio API to route signal chains. No page reloads needed, it just works perfectly across all modern browsers.
